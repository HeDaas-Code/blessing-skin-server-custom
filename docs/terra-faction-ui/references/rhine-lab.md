# Rhine Lab / 莱茵生命

Confidence: **high**. Evidence: `AK-RHINE-01`, reinforced by `AK-CV-01`.

## Contract

- Content owner: experiment, specimen, research program, or approval state.
- Default tone lane: `cool-light-laboratory`.
- Neutral field: cool-leaning laboratory white, mineral gray, charcoal.
- Default signal: chartreuse for growth, sample, or active research. Use amber/orange only when the primary real state is heat, hazard, or intervention; never use amber as the baseline identity signal.
- Type: technical sans, narrow labels, tabular specimen and time data.
- Materials: frosted diagnostic panes, brushed laboratory surfaces, safety paper, restrained glass.

## Composition

- Use translucent diagnostic planes, experiment windows, and specimen/data bands.
- Keep one approval, containment, or hazard state visually dominant.
- Let a subject image, diagram, or real chart occupy the stage; dock controls at its edge.
- Use controlled alignment and laboratory whitespace instead of a generic dark HUD.

## Components

- Experiment queue with explicit owner and stage.
- Sample or specimen strip with truthful status.
- Review dossier with hypothesis, observation, risk, and next action.
- Hazard band that appears only for a real exception.
- Versioned report or approval panel.

## Depth adaptation

- `minimal`: type, cool laboratory neutrals, one chartreuse active sample marker.
- `moderate`: add diagnostic shell and one specimen or approval band.
- `complex`: coordinate experiment stage, sample queue, dossier, alerts, dialogs, and responsive analysis panes.
- `maximal`: let live experiment state alter the diagnostic composition; degrade to a static complex layout under reduced motion.

## Avoid

- Do not turn every panel green.
- Do not use fake DNA strings, waveform noise, or hazard labels.
- Do not borrow Rhodes Island shipping-box treatment from unrelated store art.
- Do not let transparency lower text contrast.
