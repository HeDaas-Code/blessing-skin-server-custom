# Iberia / 伊比利亚

Confidence: **high**. Evidence: `AK-IBERIA-01`.

## Contract

- Content owner: voyage, tide, vessel, coastline, watch, or maritime archive.
- Default tone lane: `cold-dark-nautical`.
- Neutral field: make deep navy the dominant field; use weathered gray and parchment for reading surfaces.
- Default signal: weathered brass-gold for heading, verified route, or selected instrument.
- Type: classical serif with precise navigational sans and tabular numbers.
- Materials: engraved brass, aged paper, salt-darkened surfaces, worn painted metal.

## Composition

- Use a nautical chart, ship silhouette, compass, tide line, or voyage chronology.
- Let the selected route or vessel condition own the stage.
- Keep instruments bounded and tied to real values.
- Use Art Deco framing as hierarchy, not ornament on every card.

## Components

- Voyage chronology.
- Route and heading panel.
- Tide or watch strip with source and time.
- Vessel or archive dossier.
- Hazard state with explicit threshold and action.

## Depth adaptation

- `minimal`: dark navy field, parchment reading surface, classical title, one brass heading mark.
- `moderate`: add a chart shell and one compass or chronology layer.
- `complex`: coordinate voyage stage, route, tide, dossier, alerts, dialogs, and portrait chart flow.
- `maximal`: let course, time, and condition alter the composition while preserving a textual route summary.

## Avoid

- Do not default to cyan sci-fi sonar.
- Avoid pirate decoration, ropes, anchors, and compass roses without state.
- Do not fake coordinates, depth, tide, or vessel readings.
- Do not sacrifice table readability to aged texture.
