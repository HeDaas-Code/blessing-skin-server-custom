# Faction Element Grammar

Read this matrix on every invocation. Color, texture, typography, and animation are not sufficient faction identity by themselves. Every generated screen must contain faction-owned information elements that remain distinguishable in grayscale.

## Required element stack

At `moderate` depth and above, include all three layers:

1. **Structural identity element** — owns navigation, grouping, or the main reading surface.
2. **State instrument** — expresses a real value, chronology, threshold, ownership, or route condition.
3. **Decision artifact** — makes the primary action and its consequence legible.

At `minimal`, combine the state instrument and decision artifact when necessary, but do not remove the structural identity element.

Use real task data already present in the product. Do not invent serial numbers, coordinates, biological markers, legal seals, music notation, or tactical telemetry for atmosphere.

## Enrichment budget

Richness comes from connected information, not ornament count.

- `minimal`: do not add a secondary instrument. Combine essential state with the decision artifact.
- `moderate`: add exactly one **secondary instrument** when the task has a second useful dimension. It must own different data from the primary instrument and visibly respond to selection, progress, or commitment.
- `complex`: allow up to two secondary instruments with different owners. At least one must express a relationship, threshold, chronology, handoff, or scope boundary.
- `maximal`: orchestrate the primary and secondary instruments as a system, but keep every persistent datum under one visual owner.

Do not trigger this rule only for comparisons or paired factions. Apply it whenever one faction is generated. If the product lacks enough real data, keep the layout quieter rather than fabricating another layer.

## Composition rules

- Make the structural element occupy a bounded, stable region. It may be a rail, tab set, route gate, register, or reading surface, but it must own real information.
- A state instrument must answer at least one question: **what changed, who owns it, where is it, when is it valid, what threshold matters, or what comes next?**
- A decision artifact must show the exact state transition and any stop, rollback, expiry, or re-review condition.
- Keep one persistent datum under one visual owner. A heading scale may own `214°`; do not repeat `214°` in three unrelated badges.
- The same task may use different components by faction. A `route` screen does not force every faction into the same map card.
- For provisional families, keep the profile fixed across invocations while labeling the confidence as provisional. Do not weaken documented context corrections.

## Grayscale test

Before delivery, mentally or visually remove the palette and verify:

1. The faction is still recognizable from at least two information-bearing elements.
2. Replacing the faction name would not make the screen interchangeable with another family.
3. The identity elements still support the task instead of becoming detached decoration.
4. No cultural shorthand is doing the work of layout, state, or consequence.
