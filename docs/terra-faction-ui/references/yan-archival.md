# Yan / 炎国

Confidence: **high**. Evidence: `AK-YAN-01`.

## Contract

- Content owner: record, route, office, petition, map, or administrative decision.
- Default tone lane: `neutral-light-ink-paper`.
- Neutral field: paper white, ink black, mineral gray.
- Default signal: seal red for commitment or verification; muted gold only for rank or ceremonial emphasis. Do not cool the paper into Lungmen concrete or warm it into Laterano ivory.
- Type: readable CJK sans or serif with sparing calligraphic display.
- Materials: rice-paper texture, ink wash, architectural lattice, restrained mineral pigment.

## Composition

- Use vertical editorial labels, route/map fields, lattice boundaries, and record bands.
- Let negative space carry hierarchy.
- Use a seal only for a real verified, submitted, approved, or rejected state.
- Keep administrative clarity ahead of decorative tradition.

## Components

- Record index.
- Route or district map with legend.
- Petition or report dossier.
- Chronology or correspondence band.
- Commit action with clear status and consequence.

## Depth adaptation

- `minimal`: paper/ink hierarchy and one seal-red state.
- `moderate`: add an archival shell and one route, lattice, or record layer.
- `complex`: coordinate map stage, records, dossier, correspondence, dialogs, and portrait editorial flow.
- `maximal`: let route or administrative state recompose the page while preserving a plain reading view.

## Avoid

- Do not substitute generic red and gold.
- Do not use random calligraphy, cloud motifs, dragons, or faux classical text.
- Do not use vertical text for essential instructions.
- Do not put a seal on every card or control.
