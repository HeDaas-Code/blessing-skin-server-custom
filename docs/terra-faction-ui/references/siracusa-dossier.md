# Siracusa / 叙拉古

Confidence: **high**. Evidence: `AK-SIRACUSA-01`.

## Contract

- Content owner: evidence, testimony, proceeding, relationship, or ruling.
- Default tone lane: `warm-mid-dossier`.
- Neutral field: near-black, cream, walnut.
- Default signal: burgundy for active proceedings; rare judicial red for exception. Keep cream/walnut as the dominant dossier field rather than Laterano-like ivory/gold or Leithanien-like dark burgundy.
- Type: editorial serif for headings, readable sans for evidence and controls.
- Materials: wood, leather, paper, restrained noir lighting.

## Composition

- Use a case-file rail, courtroom elevation, witness sequence, or evidence chronology.
- Let the selected proceeding or testimony own the stage.
- Separate allegation, evidence, testimony, and ruling.
- Use stamps or crests only as state owners, never as repeated wallpaper.

## Components

- Case docket.
- Witness or party list.
- Evidence sequence with provenance.
- Relationship map with a textual alternative.
- Ruling or next-action panel.

## Depth adaptation

- `minimal`: cream/near-black editorial hierarchy and one burgundy active rule.
- `moderate`: add a case-file rail and one evidence or testimony layer.
- `complex`: coordinate courtroom stage, docket, evidence, relationships, dialogs, and responsive reading modes.
- `maximal`: let proceeding phase recompose the dossier while preserving a linear accessible record.

## Avoid

- Avoid mafia clichés, casino motifs, pinstripes, decorative guns, and red-string boards.
- Do not style accusations as facts.
- Do not use noir contrast that obscures evidence text.
- Do not invent crests, case numbers, or witnesses merely for atmosphere.
