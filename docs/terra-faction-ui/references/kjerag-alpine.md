# Kjerag / 谢拉格

Confidence: **high**. Evidence: `AK-KJERAG-01`, reinforced by the early section in `AK-CV-01`.

## Contract

- Content owner: alpine route, weather window, rail state, expedition, or ceremonial record.
- Default tone lane: `cold-mid-alpine`.
- Neutral field: ice blue-white, slate, cold gray.
- Default signal: teal for route, weather, or safe passage. Use brass only when the primary real state is ceremonial.
- Type: tall serif contrast with compact travel and weather labels.
- Materials: textile edge, carved metal, weathered paper, clear cold glass.

## Composition

- Use a route or rail line, weather band, elevation field, or ceremonial dossier.
- Let terrain and safe passage own the stage.
- Use woven geometry at boundaries, not across every surface.
- Balance expansive snow-field whitespace with compact operational data.

## Components

- Route and stop sequence.
- Weather window with source and update time.
- Expedition or traveler dossier.
- Ceremony or permit record.
- Safety action with explicit threshold and consequence.

## Depth adaptation

- `minimal`: cold neutrals, tall title, one teal or brass route marker.
- `moderate`: add an alpine shell and one route or weather layer.
- `complex`: coordinate terrain stage, route, weather, dossier, alerts, and portrait travel flow.
- `maximal`: let altitude, weather, or route selection recompose the stage with static fallbacks.

## Avoid

- Do not use snow particles as perpetual filler.
- Do not confuse event-wide winter packaging with stable Kjerag identity.
- Do not add generic Nordic runes.
- Do not hide safety thresholds in decorative weather graphics.
