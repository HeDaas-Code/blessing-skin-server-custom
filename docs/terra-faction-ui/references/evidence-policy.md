# Evidence and reuse policy

## Confidence classes

- **Direct**: visibly present in an official rendered page or its public CSS.
- **Repeated**: present in at least two relevant official images.
- **Supported inference**: a composition or material rule derived from repeated direct evidence.
- **Provisional inference**: based on one early or context-limited official surface.

Promote a motif into a faction recipe only when it is repeated or independently supported. Treat generic website chrome, unrelated fashion banners, recruitment panels, store packaging, and shared Arknights marketing components as contamination unless the faction owns the pattern.

## Asset classes

### Official production evidence

Public HTML, CSS, JavaScript, images, fonts, audio, and video are not automatically open source. Analyze and cite them. Do not bundle or redistribute them without an explicit license or user-provided rights.

### Open reusable assets

Bundle only files with verified compatible licenses. Preserve the original license and attribution. A repository license does not grant rights to third-party game art embedded in it.

### Clean-room output

Write new tokens, layouts, code, SVG geometry, textures, and components from high-level observations. Do not trace logos, faction marks, character silhouettes, screenshots, or image-baked UI.

### User-supplied assets

Use supplied official assets only within the user's stated rights and distribution scope. Ask for rights confirmation when redistribution is material.

## Evidence workflow

1. Identify the exact faction and screen task.
2. Read its recipe and evidence IDs.
3. Separate direct observation from inference in notes.
4. Choose semantic roles before colors: field, ink, surface, rule, signal, critical.
5. Generate original compositions and geometry.
6. Cite official URLs in project documentation when the design claim matters.
7. Re-check live sources before making time-sensitive claims.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Do not download private endpoints, source maps, cookies, credentials, or gated media.
- Do not copy public production CSS/JS into output code.
- Do not use fan redesigns as official faction evidence.
- Do not label sampled colors as official brand tokens.
- Do not infer a whole culture from one character outfit or one promotional campaign.
