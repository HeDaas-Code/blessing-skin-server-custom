# Laterano / 拉特兰

Confidence: **high**. Evidence: `AK-LATERANO-01`.

## Contract

- Content owner: case, judgment, covenant, approval, or exception.
- Default tone lane: `warm-light-ceremonial`.
- Neutral field: ivory, charcoal, warm stone.
- Default signal: brass-gold for authority or confirmation; restrained red only for exception or judgment.
- Type: serif display with disciplined sans body and labels.
- Materials: carved stone, parchment, fine metal rules, archival paper.

## Composition

- Use radial halo or clock geometry as a bounded instrument, not universal chrome.
- Structure cases with notarial bands, seal states, and formal archive hierarchy.
- Let whitespace and measured symmetry express authority; break symmetry only for a current exception.
- Use fine rules and stately vertical rhythm.

## Components

- Case index and case dossier.
- Approval or covenant status.
- Chronology with verified dates.
- Evidence or party list.
- Formal commit action with explicit consequence.

## Depth adaptation

- `minimal`: ivory/charcoal contrast, serif title, one gold approval rule.
- `moderate`: add formal archive shell and one radial or seal instrument.
- `complex`: coordinate case stage, chronology, evidence, approval states, dialogs, and portrait archive flow.
- `maximal`: let case status and chronology reconfigure the instrument; preserve a quiet reading mode.

## Avoid

- Do not make every control circular.
- Do not use halos as decoration on unrelated controls.
- Do not invent Latin, legal codes, seals, or scripture-like copy.
- Do not turn gold into a large low-contrast text field.
