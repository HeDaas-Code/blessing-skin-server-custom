# Penguin Logistics street dispatch

Key: `penguin-logistics-street`

## Contract

```text
faction: penguin-logistics-street
confidence: medium
default depth: moderate
task owner: dispatch, courier route, delivery, or handoff state
tone lane: cool-dark-navy-white
evidence pattern: night-city field + hard white waybill + diagonal handoff lane
primary action: commit the selected parcel to the named next courier
```

This is an evidence-bounded clean-room grammar, not an official token set. `complex` and `maximal` are allowed when the product has enough real route, parcel, and handoff state to support the added layers.

## Evidence boundary

- `AK-CV-01` directly shows an early saturated-blue Penguin Logistics section with layered media.
- `AK-PENGUIN-02` is the official `Code of Brawl` announcement/PV. Across the faction-owned event surface, night-city fields, cold blue/ink navy, hard white information framing, and movement through urban space recur.
- `AK-PENGUIN-03` independently confirms the Penguin Logistics safehouse and event context, but reuses the same campaign and must not be counted as a new palette sample.
- `MSR-PENGUIN-01` reinforces night, map, direction, forward movement, deep blue-black, paper white, and a small cold-blue device field.
- Promote the route, handoff, urban-night, and white-document relationships. Do not promote character colors, the event reward yellow, graffiti, or generic official-site chrome.

## Visual grammar

- Field: ink navy or blue-black, with cold blue reserved for route depth and subordinate mapping.
- Signal: paper white owns the active waybill, selection, route handoff, and successful commitment.
- Critical: restrained red only for a missed window, damaged parcel, or failed handoff.
- Materials: removable waybills, perforated ticket edges, dark street glass, lane paint, and layered dispatch slips.
- Type: compact sans labels, tabular times and distances, one hard white hierarchy band. Avoid a music-poster or nightlife-ad composition.

## Composition and information

- Structural owner: a diagonal handoff lane crossing a dark city field.
- Navigation: ticket stack placed on the route; it must feel removable rather than like a left sidebar.
- Primary instrument: a waybill stub owning origin, destination, segment, time, distance, and next courier.
- Secondary instrument: parcel condition tags owning handling requirements and the next handoff window.
- Decision artifact: a full-width handoff strip at the next courier edge, with exact prerequisites and load/window consequences.
- At maximal depth, add route chronology or parcel-to-courier relationship, not extra stickers.

## Interaction and motion

- Primary control: removable waybill with small ticket notches or perforated sides.
- Binary control: ticket-cut checkbox whose selected state gains a paper-white dispatch band.
- Continuous control: broad waybill slider for a real limit such as load or handoff tolerance.
- Hover/press: move slightly along the route axis; commitment snaps the selected stub into the next lane.
- Focus: a white double perforation or clearly offset ticket silhouette.
- Arrival: diagonal waybill snap; instrument motion advances only between real handoff nodes.

## Avoid

- Electric blue as the only identity cue, yellow baseline accents, sticker noise, generic graffiti, hip-hop stereotypes, or toy courier controls.
- Invented tracking numbers, fake GPS telemetry, decorative city coordinates, or delivery claims not owned by real data.
- Reusing Blacksteel's equipment rack, Lungmen's civic gate, or Kazimierz's broadcast frame.

## Grayscale identifiers

The interface must remain identifiable through at least two of:

1. removable perforated waybill;
2. diagonal handoff lane with ordered courier nodes;
3. full-width next-courier commitment strip;
4. parcel-condition tags attached to the selected slip.
